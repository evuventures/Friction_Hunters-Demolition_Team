# Friction Hunters - Demolition Team

**A collection of small, powerful internal tools built by the EVU team to hunt down and destroy cognitive latency in our workflows.**

## Mission Statement

Our goal is to build the most efficient and intelligent work environment in the world, by us and for us. We are a team of world-class AI builders, and it's time we became our own best customer. This repository is the home of that movement.

Our mission is simple: **Hunt and destroy friction.** We identify anything that wastes our time and build a smart, simple solution to eliminate it forever. Every tool in this repository is a victory against wasted equity and a step towards a frictionless future.

## The Problem: The Enemy Inside the Walls

We are fighting **Cognitive Latency**—the gap between the speed of our thoughts and the speed of our tools. This enemy manifests in two ways:

1. **The Craftsman's Paradox:** Using generic, inefficient third-party tools for tasks we can solve better ourselves (e.g., clunky PDF editors, manual data entry).
2. **Communication Gaps:** Wasting time in meetings or messages due to misaligned expectations, unclear requirements, or lost information.

## How to Contribute

Everyone at EVU has a critical role to play. This movement is divided into two teams:

### 🏹 The Friction Hunters (Everyone)

Your job is to be the eyes and ears of the company. You find the enemy.

* **Mission:** When you encounter a repetitive task, a frustrating workflow, or a moment of confusion, **document it**.
* **Action:** Post a screenshot, screen recording, or a clear description of the problem in our **"Friction Hunters" WhatsApp group**.
* **Motto:** *You don't need to know the solution. Your only job is to notice the problem.*

### ⚡️ The Friction Destroyers (Builders)

Your job is to be the immune system. You see a problem and eliminate it.

* **Mission:** Monitor the WhatsApp group. When you see a piece of friction you can solve, claim it.
* **Action:**
   1. Reply **"Mine"** to the message in the WhatsApp group.
   2. Create a new folder in this repository for your tool.
   3. Build the simplest possible solution (a single HTML file, a Python script, etc.).
   4. Add a brief README.md inside your tool's folder explaining what it does.
   5. Once complete, link your solution back to the original WhatsApp message.
